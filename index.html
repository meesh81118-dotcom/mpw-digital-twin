How the twin works

The twin runs, in the browser, the same equations as the Python codes of Appendix H: the split of the clean syngas at D-102 and the water-gas shift of Chapter 2, the membrane ME-101 with the plug-flow Sieverts model of Section 3.5, the synthesis loop with its recycle and the cash-flow model of Chapter 8 (Appendix G). One evaluation takes less than one millisecond, so the AI buttons can run a full differential-evolution search (2,664 evaluations) in a few seconds. The design limits are those of Section 10.2: R-102 at its design load (fixed catalyst charge), the installed membrane area of ME-101, and the R-101 feed and the other units 5 % above their design loads. In safe operation every unit stays below its design load by the selected margin.
