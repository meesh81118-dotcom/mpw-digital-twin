300,000t/ymixed plastic waste in 1,200 °Csteam gasification 266,915t/ymethanol, 99.5 mol% 61,831t/yhydrogen, 99.99 %

A complete gasification complex, from the shredders to the 52-tray methanol column, with a digital twin that runs the plant model, the heat-integration network, the control loops and the economics live in your browser.

3D presentation

One plot, five process sections

The process area as built in the 3D model of the plant, drawn as a technical isometric. Select a tag to open its data sheet on the flowsheet.

Live readout

Current state of the twin with the set point, the market and the heat-integration choice of the other sheets.

From plastic bale to methanol, in five steps

Each section is designed in detail in the report and runs in the twin. The line between the steps is the main process stream, numbered as on the flowsheet.
